SECTION 1: ARRIVAL & BIOMETRICS (OAMP/MOI)
Source: Ministry of the Interior (MVČR) - Official 2026 Guidelines

1. REPORTING ARRIVAL (Non-EU)
   - Deadline: Within 3 working days of entering the Czech Republic.
   - Action: All foreign students must report in person to the Ministry of the Interior (MOI/OAMP) office.
   - Purpose: Collection of biometric data (facial image + fingerprints).

2. RESIDENCE CARD COLLECTION
   - Students must collect their biometric residence card within 60 days of the data collection. Failure to collect the card may lead to the cancellation of the residence permit.

3. FOREIGN POLICE REGISTRATION (EU & Non-EU)
   - Non-EU Students: Mandatory registration within 3 working days (usually handled at the OAMP during biometrics).
   - EU Students: Must register with the Foreign Police within 30 days if staying longer than 30 days.

SECTION 2: EXTENDING YOUR RESIDENCE PERMIT
Source: Act No. 326/1999 Coll. on the Residence of Foreign Nationals

1. FILING WINDOWS (Strict Deadlines)
   - Earliest: 90 days before current permit expiration.
   - Latest: Last day of current permit validity (Note: Previously it was 14 days, but currently the MOI accepts applications until the last day to prevent illegal stay).
   - Recommendation: Apply at least 30 days before expiry to avoid processing delays.

2. THE "BRIDGE LABEL" (Překlenovací štítek)
   - Purpose: If your visa expires while an extension is being processed, you must request this physical stamp in your passport at the OAMP office. 
   - Travel: It allows you to travel outside the Czech Republic and re-enter legally during the "pending" period.

SECTION 3: FINANCIAL REQUIREMENTS (PROOF OF FUNDS 2026)
Source: MVČR - Official 2026 Existential Minimum Standards

1. CALCULATION FORMULA (Existential Minimum = 3,130 CZK)
   - First Month: 15 times the existential minimum (15 x 3,130 = 46,950 CZK).
   - Each Additional Month: 2 times the existential minimum (2 x 3,130 = 6,260 CZK).

2. REQUIRED TOTAL AMOUNTS FOR 2026/2027
   - 1 Semester (6 months): 78,250 CZK (approx. €3,100 / $3,350).
   - 1 Academic Year (12 months): 115,810 CZK (approx. €4,600 / $5,000).
   - Minors (Under 18): Only required to prove 50% of these amounts.

3. ACCEPTED PROOF
   - Personal Bank Statement: Must be in the student's name + Copy of an international debit card (Visa/Mastercard).
   - Scholarship Confirmation: Must state the specific monthly amount.
   - Sponsorship: Notarized letter + Sponsor's bank statements + Official Czech translation.

SECTION 4: HEALTH INSURANCE & DOCUMENTS
Source: Ministry of Health & MVČR Requirements

1. COMPREHENSIVE INSURANCE (Mandatory)
   - Only "Comprehensive Healthcare Insurance" (Komplexní pojištění) is accepted for long-term stays.
   - Authorized Providers: Pojišťovna VZP (PVZP) is the primary provider. "Emergency" or foreign insurance is NOT valid for extensions inside the CR.

2. DOCUMENT VALIDITY
   - All supporting documents (Bank statements, Study confirmations, Accommodation contracts) must NOT be older than 180 days at the time of submission.
   - Passports must be valid for at least 3 months beyond the intended stay.

SECTION 5: ADMINISTRATIVE EXPULSION & NEW 2026 RULES
Source: Act No. 326/1999 Coll. (Amended 2026)

1. REPEATED OFFENCES (New for 2026)
   - Effective Jan 1, 2026, a residence permit can be revoked or not extended if the foreigner repeatedly commits administrative offences (e.g., disturbing public order, noise violations, or minor traffic offences). "Repeated" is defined as 3 or more offences within 12 months.

2. GROUNDS FOR DEPORTATION
   - Invalid residence (overstaying).
   - Forged documents.
   - Illegal employment (working without being a student).
   - Failure to meet the "Purpose of Stay" (e.g., expelled from university).

3. THE EXIT ORDER (Výjezdní příkaz)
   - Validity: Usually 30 days.
   - Constraint: You CANNOT travel to other Schengen countries. It is for leaving the CR only.

SECTION 6: LEGAL APPEALS & DATA BOX
Source: Information Portal for Foreigners (ipc.gov.cz)

1. FILING AN APPEAL (Rejection)
   - Deadline: Exactly 15 days from the day after the decision was delivered.
   - Suspensive Effect: An appeal usually allows you to stay in the CR until the decision is final, PROVIDED it was a renewal/extension application.

2. THE "DATA BOX" (Datová schránka)
   - Highly recommended for students (Free at Czech POINT).
   - Legal Fact: Messages are considered "delivered" 10 days after sent, even if not opened.
   - Benefit: Instant notification of missing documents or approval.

SECTION 7: POST-GRADUATION (JOB SEEKING)
   - Graduates of Czech universities can apply for a 9-month residence permit to find a job or start a business.
   - Requirements: Diploma/Study completion certificate + Proof of funds (SECTION 3) + Insurance (SECTION 4).
